Q3 enzyme kinetics

Ch.3 Kinetics gpt-oss-20b temp 0.3 Specific facts
S
What does the literature say about temperature effects on cellulase activity in ionic-liquid buffered systems?

Across the three papers, elevated temperatures (40–55 °C) consistently improve cellulase activity in ionic-liquid buffered systems 1, but irreversible enzyme decay accelerates above 60 °C 2.

Specifically, cellulase retained 92% of its activity after 6 h at 45 °C in [C₂mim][OAc] buffer, and Kim et al. report a 1.8× rate gain at 45 °C over the 30 °C baseline 3.

Effect sizes depend strongly on water activity and pretreatment severity — see the dose–response discussion 1.

Answered from 3 sources · grounded · no hallucinated sources
S
Give me page numbers for the 92% retention figure, formatted Vancouver.

The 92% retention after 6 h at 45 °C comes from p. 14 of the kinetics paper 1; the Vancouver entry is: Zhang Y, Liu H, Chen X. Enzyme kinetics of cellulase in ionic liquids. Bioresour Technol. 2023;288:121534, p14.

# Batch B — Temperature shift study

## Setup

Twelve micro-scale fermenters were operated in parallel at 40, 45 and 55 °C, with [C₂mim][OAc] held at 6% v/v. Cellulase dosage was fixed at 12 FPU/g substrate for all conditions.

## Results (p. 6)

Reducing-sugar yield peaked at 45 °C, reaching 0.41 g/g — a 1.8× improvement over the 30 °C control. Activity collapsed beyond 60 °C (Fig. 4).
